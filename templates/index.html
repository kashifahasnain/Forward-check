      What does “Unverified” mean?

      Unverified means Forward Check did not have enough independently retrieved evidence for a verdict. It does not mean the claim is false. Gemma's assessment is an AI opinion, not source verification.

      Warning Signals

      Verification Steps

        Fake Voice and Video Warning

        AI-generated or manipulated audio and video can sound or look convincing. Unusual visual details or a suspicious-sounding voice alone do not prove that media is fake. Forward Check does not detect or verify deepfakes.

        • Find out who first posted the content and check the original source.
        • Look for the full video or recording instead of relying on a short clip.
        • Check whether trustworthy news organizations or official sources independently confirm the claim.
        • Take a relevant video frame and reverse-search it with Google Lens or Bing Visual Search. Avoid uploading private or sensitive images.
        • For a suspicious voice message, contact the person using a phone number you already know or another trusted channel.
        • Be cautious about urgent requests for money, passwords, OTPs, or confidential information.
        • Do not forward audio or video until you have checked it.
